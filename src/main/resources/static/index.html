Spire Emissions Calculator is a voluntary, non-profit software initiative developed to model and simulate critical Volatile Organic Compound (VOC) emission hotspots across urban environments.

By leveraging a bespoke Artificial Intelligence engine developed specifically for this project, the system predicts potential pollution spikes and unveils underlying risk factors. This allows local authorities and health specialists to take proactive measures to mitigate atmospheric toxicity and safeguard public health.

🔬 Scientific Rigour & Traceability All mathematical formulas, empirical constants, and baseline datasets used in this simulator are rigorously derived from peer-reviewed scientific literature and official governmental health agencies, ensuring complete transparency and data traceability.
